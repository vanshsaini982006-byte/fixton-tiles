About {{brand}}

A tiles business that wants buying tiles to be easier than walking from shop to shop.

The problem we are fixing

Tiles are hard to buy online. Designs are difficult to compare, product details are thin, and most people end up visiting a showroom just to find out what is available. Small tile businesses also struggle to be found online at all.

What this site does

  • A digital catalogue you can search and filter by size, colour, finish, material, price and application.
  • Room-wise suggestions, so you can see what suits a bathroom floor, a kitchen splashback or a staircase.
  • Quick enquiries through a form or WhatsApp, with a Get a Quote button on every tile.
  • Customer reviews and a store finder, so you can buy with some confidence.

How it works for you

Browse, filter and compare. Tell us the area and we send a quote. Visit a showroom if you want to see the real tile. Then order, and we arrange delivery.
